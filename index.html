We recently tested 21 AI models with our Investment Research Benchmark (IRB). Kimi K3.1 Pro scored 93.53 and tied for second. Its per-task cost was about a fifth of the cost for the highest-scoring models in the same run. The task-level results were less tidy. Kimi reconstructed a raw-material self-sufficiency rate from figures scattered across filings, then ran a margin sensitivity. It scored 97.0 after challenging a loaded question about an export tax rebate cut. In another test, it used US domestic production instead of global production as the denominator, giving 212% instead of 24%. It scored 50.
